Privacy policy

Google Drive data

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. Google limits this permission to files authorized for the utility and files it creates. The utility reads the approved file's metadata and contents to verify delivery. A separately authorized validation can use a disposable test file.

Use, storage and sharing

The utility exchanges authorization data and Drive requests with Google. It processes the selected Drive data on systems controlled by the owner. Authorization credentials are kept in access-restricted storage. The utility keeps Drive content out of this public site and uses it only to validate or deliver the approved handoff.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke access in the Google Account connections settings and remove the saved authorization from the system running the utility. The owner manages retention and deletion of files in Google Drive.

Contact

For questions about the utility or its handling of data, use the support contact displayed on Google's authorization screen.
